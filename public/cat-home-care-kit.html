6 · Safety reminder

Call a veterinarian promptly for trouble breathing, collapse, severe bleeding, suspected poisoning, repeated vomiting, inability to urinate, or any sudden change that worries you. If you think your cat has been poisoned, do not induce vomiting unless a veterinary professional tells you to; call your veterinarian or a pet poison service right away.
